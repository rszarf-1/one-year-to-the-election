How the numbers work

Tarna is not a real country and the numbers are invented. They are simple on purpose. Only the directions come from research:

  • Money that goes only to poor families cuts poverty more per crown than money for everyone.
  • Voters notice cash more easily than tax rules.
  • Many voters support conditions such as work.
  • People feel a loss more strongly than a gain.

The simulation counts only two things: child poverty and votes. Real reforms have more effects, for example on work, prices and the state budget.
